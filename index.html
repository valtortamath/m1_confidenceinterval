HKDSE M1 Exam Key Takeaway & Interpretation Rules 🎯

Must-know concepts for HKDSE M1 Paper 1 Section A & B written questions

Critical Exam Focus
1. What "95% Confidence" REALLY Means

In HKDSE M1, the true population mean μ is a FIXED constant, NOT a random variable.

If we repeat the sampling process 100 times, approximately 95 of the constructed intervals will contain μ.

2. The Classic DSE Trap to Avoid

Once a specific interval (e.g. (45.2, 52.8)) is calculated, it either contains μ or it does not.

❌ NEVER say: "There is a 95% probability that μ lies inside (45.2, 52.8)." (0 marks in DSE!)

HKDSE Written Question Phrase Checker
CORRECT DSE Phrasing:

"We are 95% confident that the true population mean μ lies between 45.2 and 52.8."

WRONG DSE Phrasing (Lose Marks):

"The probability that μ lies between 45.2 and 52.8 is 0.95."

HKDSE Quadrupling Rule:

To halve the width of a confidence interval (cut error by 2), you must quadruple (4×) the sample size n!
